> Here, you can freely share your personal opinions about your experience participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. This will help the FCAJ team improve any shortcomings based on the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is high-quality, cool, and comfortable, which helps me focus better. There's an event every week with diverse, engaging content, giving me the chance to gain extra knowledge and experiences beyond my main tasks.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when I don’t understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The admin team is friendly and enthusiastic, always ready to support and answer any questions I have. I especially appreciate that the mentor allows me to try and solve problems myself instead of just giving the answer.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks I was assigned align well with the knowledge I learned at university — specifically, I took a course called "Open Source Software Development," which included an introduction to AWS cloud services and helped me get up to speed with the internship work faster. The internship also introduced me to new areas I had never encountered before. This allowed me to both strengthen my foundational knowledge and gain practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the internship, I learned new skills such as teamwork and professional communication in a corporate environment. My team held its own weekly meetings to review progress and divide tasks for the following week, which helped me build stronger time-management and team-coordination skills.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously but still keeps things enjoyable. We shared the workspace with several other intern teams from different universities; everyone brought their own essentials (power strips, bottled water, etc.) and strictly followed the office's shared rules. Even with over 100 people working at the same time, the office always stayed quiet, which made it easy to focus. This made me feel like a real part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company offers flexible working hours when needed, which helped me balance my university coursework with the internship.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
  What I found most satisfying was building a complete product from scratch — from a simple RAG chatbot idea to a real system running on AWS with full authentication, CI/CD, and monitoring. When I hit a tricky bug like the multi-tenancy data leak, I researched it and found that it was already a known issue in the AWS community, with official documentation and a recommended fix — reading through that, applying the right fix, and then turning it into a blog post for the AWS Study Group community was the most memorable part.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  
  I think the program could create more opportunities for interaction between my team and other intern teams sharing the same office (from different universities), instead of each team working independently in its own space.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
  Yes. I'd recommend it because you get to work on a real project with the latest AWS services — for example, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and the new Amazon Nova models released in 2026, or the newly updated AWS architecture icon set in Draw.io — with a mentor who guides you closely but still lets you solve problems on your own. This let me both work with cutting-edge technology and learn a lot in a short time.

### Suggestions & Expectations
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?  
  Maybe add 1-2 career-focused sharing sessions from senior AWS staff, in addition to the purely technical sessions.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?  
  Yes, if given the chance I'd love to continue with similar FCAJ or AWS programs in the future, even in a different role such as mentoring future interns.
- Any other comments (free sharing):
  Thank you to the FCAJ team and my mentor for giving me the chance to learn and practice in an environment that's professional yet still very friendly. This has been a memorable internship experience.
